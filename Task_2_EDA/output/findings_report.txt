TASK 2 - EDA FINDINGS REPORT

Dataset
This project uses a retail sales dataset containing 1,200 order records.
The dataset was created for this exercise because no dataset was supplied by
the internship company.

Data quality
Duplicate rows: 0
Missing values are checked in the analysis and retained for inspection.

Key figures
Total sales: 1,372,915.12
Total profit: 385,431.61
Average order value: 1,144.10
Overall profit margin: 28.07%

Business questions
1. Highest-sales product:
   Laptop (543,715.20 sales)

2. Highest-sales region:
   North (368,990.52 sales)

3. Highest-sales month:
   2025-12 (148,403.27 sales)

4. Relationship between sales and profit:
   Pearson correlation = 0.968.
   Higher sales generally occur with higher profit in this dataset.

Outlier analysis
The 1.5 x IQR rule was applied to Quantity, Sales and Profit.
See outlier_summary.csv for the thresholds and outliers.csv for the records.

Main observations
- Product and regional performance varies across the dataset.
- Monthly sales show changes in demand over the year.
- Sales are spread across a range of order values, with unusually large
  transactions visible in the distribution and boxplot.
- Sales and profit have a positive relationship.
- The generated charts provide visual support for the numerical findings.
